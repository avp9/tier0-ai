# Glossary

## Ticket
A support request raised on behalf of an Employee and tracked by the support team in the ticketing platform. Has an identifier, title, description, status, and updates from the support team.
_Not to be confused with:_ backlog items, issues, or pull requests used to develop Tier0 itself.

## Requester Email
The email address identifying the Employee who raised a Ticket. Always the signed-in Employee's email; an Employee can only see Tickets with their own Requester Email.

## Employee
A BuzzTech Solutions staff member who signs in to Tier0 to ask an IT, HR, Onboarding, or Payroll question.

## Knowledge Gap
A topic deliberately absent from the knowledge base, so questions about it must lead to an offer to raise a Ticket rather than an answer.
