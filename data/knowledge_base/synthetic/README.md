# Synthetic BuzzTech content

Everything in this folder is **fictional**. BuzzTech Solutions does not exist, and the leave allowances, pay dates, amounts, notice periods and contact roles are invented for this capstone. They are not real company policy and must not be treated as advice.

| File | Category | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| `onboarding.json` | Onboarding | 12 |
| `payroll.json` | Payroll & Compensation | 15 |
| `hr_policy.json` | HR Policy | 29 |

Each entry has `document_id`, `title`, `category`, `source`, `content`, `last_updated` and `synthetic: true`.

Rules for editing:

- Keep facts consistent across entries (for example the payroll cut-off on the 20th, the 60-day notice period, and the 30-day benefits window are each referenced from several entries).
- Do not cover a Knowledge Gap topic from `data/evaluation/knowledge_gaps.json`; a test enforces this.
- Do not add URLs, real email addresses or phone numbers.
